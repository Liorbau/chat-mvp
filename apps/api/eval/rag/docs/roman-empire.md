# The Roman Empire

The Roman Empire was the post-Republican period of ancient Rome. It began in
27 BC when Augustus became the first emperor after the fall of the Roman
Republic.

## Expansion and rule

At its height under Trajan in 117 AD, the empire stretched from Britain to the
Persian Gulf and surrounded the Mediterranean Sea. Roman roads, aqueducts, and
law spread across these territories.

## Decline

The empire split into Western and Eastern halves. The Western Roman Empire fell
in 476 AD when the Germanic leader Odoacer deposed the emperor Romulus
Augustulus. The Eastern (Byzantine) Empire continued for nearly a thousand years.
