# The Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves continuously between the atmosphere,
land, and oceans. It is driven by energy from the sun.

## Main stages

Evaporation turns liquid water from oceans and lakes into water vapor.
Transpiration releases water vapor from plants. Condensation forms clouds as
vapor cools. Precipitation returns water to the surface as rain or snow.
Collection gathers water back into rivers, lakes, and groundwater.

## Why it matters

The water cycle redistributes fresh water across the planet and regulates
temperature. Most of Earth's water is salt water in the oceans; only a small
fraction is fresh water available to land ecosystems.
